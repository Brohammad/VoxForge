## 1. Billing contact changes

Customers can update the billing contact on their account.

Requirements:
- Account email must be verified.
- Requester must be an account owner or billing admin.
- New billing contact needs a valid work email.

Process:
1. Verify the caller's identity with account email + last invoice ID.
2. Confirm the new billing contact name and email.
3. Update the billing contact in the billing portal.
4. Send a confirmation email to both the old and new contacts.

Scripted reply:
"I can help with that. After I verify your account, I'll update the billing contact and send a confirmation email."

Typical resolution time: under 5 minutes when identity is verified.

## 2. Refund policy

Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase when:
- The order ID is provided.
- The subscription has not already been refunded.
- Usage charges for the current cycle are under the published fair-use cap.

Refunds are not available for:
- Custom professional services already delivered.
- Third-party marketplace add-ons billed by another vendor.
- Accounts suspended for abuse.

How to request:
1. Collect order ID and account email.
2. Confirm purchase date is within 30 days.
3. Submit refund ticket with reason code REFUND_30D.
4. Tell the customer refunds post in 5–7 business days.

## 3. Support hours

Billing support (phone / voice):
- Monday–Friday, 9:00–18:00 IST
- Closed on Indian public holidays

Technical support (chat / ticket):
- 24×7 for Enterprise plans
- Business hours for Starter and Pro

Emergency production outages:
- Always escalate to the on-call SRE rotation.

---

## 4. When to escalate to a human

Escalate immediately if:
- The caller asks for a human agent.
- Identity cannot be verified after two attempts.
- The issue involves a disputed charge over $1,000.
- The customer reports unauthorized account access.
- Confidence is low or the knowledge base has no matching article.

Handoff checklist:
- Capture account email.
- Capture order ID / invoice ID if available.
- Summarize what was already tried.
- Keep the caller on the line until an agent accepts.

## 5. Common intents

| Intent | Example phrase | First action |
|--------|----------------|--------------|
| billing_contact_change | "I need to change the billing contact" | Verify identity, update contact |
| refund_request | "I want a refund for order …" | Check 30-day window |
| invoice_copy | "Can you resend last month's invoice?" | Lookup invoice and email PDF |
| plan_upgrade | "We want to move to Enterprise" | Collect seats + region, create sales ticket |
| password_reset | "I can't log in" | Send secure reset link; do not ask for password |

## 6. Tone and safety

- Be concise and calm.
- Never invent policy that is not in this document.
- Never ask for full credit card numbers over voice.
- If unsure, say you will escalate rather than guess.
